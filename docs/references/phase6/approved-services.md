## GeM Portal Services

### Seller Registration & Profile Setup

Start your government business journey with a well-prepared GeM seller account. We assist with registration, document coordination and profile setup to showcase your business on the portal.

### Product / Service Catalogue Upload

Present your products and services clearly to government buyers. We support category selection, specification entry, document uploads and catalogue creation to reflect your offerings accurately.

### Bid Search & Analysis

Identify opportunities that match your business capabilities. We review relevant bids, eligibility criteria, technical requirements and submission timelines to help you make informed participation decisions.

### Tender Participation & Support

Manage tender submissions with organised documentation and timely coordination. We assist with bid preparation, document uploads, submission and clarification responses, helping reduce avoidable errors.

### OEM Authorization & Vendor Assessment

Navigate OEM authorization requirements and vendor assessment with structured guidance. We assist with document preparation, application coordination and follow-ups based on the requirements applicable to your business.

### Catalogue Approval / Rejection Support

Resolve catalogue issues with a clear understanding of the required corrections. We review rejection remarks, identify gaps and assist with updates and resubmission to support the approval process.

### Order & Contract Support

Stay organised after receiving a GeM order. We assist with order acceptance, contract requirement tracking, delivery coordination and portal invoicing, helping you manage commitments and follow-ups.

## HR Support Services

### HR Audit & HR Process Setup

Understand where your HR practices need improvement. We review existing processes, records and documentation, identify gaps and establish practical HR systems that support your business as it grows.

### Recruitment & Talent Acquisition

Find people who fit your roles and business needs. We support job descriptions, candidate sourcing, screening, interview coordination and selection follow-ups to make hiring more structured and efficient.

### Payroll & Statutory Compliance

Simplify monthly payroll with coordinated attendance inputs, salary calculations and payslip preparation. We also support applicable statutory compliance activities and coordinate with your finance team or compliance specialists.

### HR Policies & Documentation

Give employees and managers clear guidelines for working together. We develop practical HR policies, employee handbooks, appointment letters and other essential documents tailored to your organisation’s needs.

### PMS / KRA-KPI

Connect individual performance with business goals. We help define Key Result Areas and Key Performance Indicators, establish appraisal processes and support performance reviews that bring clarity to expectations and accountability.

### Employee Engagement & Retention

Build a workplace where employees feel heard, valued and connected. We support feedback initiatives, recognition programmes and engagement activities, helping you identify employee concerns and strengthen retention efforts.

### Onboarding, Exit & Full & Final Settlement

Manage employee transitions with clear processes and complete documentation. We support joining formalities, induction coordination, exit procedures, clearance tracking and full and final settlement calculations for a smoother employee experience.
